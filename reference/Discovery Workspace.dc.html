Order exceptions are a coordination problem, not a routing problem

Across twelve interviews the routing engine was never named as the cause of a missed delivery window. What people described instead was a queue that can report a failure but cannot ask anyone to fix it — so the fix migrates to spreadsheets, phone calls and texts, where it becomes invisible.
